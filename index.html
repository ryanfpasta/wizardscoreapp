Wizard Score

Scorekeeper for the Wizard card game

Download for Android (APK)

Version 1.1 · Android 7.0 or newer · about 480 KB

Install

  1. Open this page on your Android phone and tap Download.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If asked, allow installs from your browser.
  3. Tap Install.

How scoring works

  • 2 to 6 players. Rounds are 60 divided by the number of players.
  • Each round, enter every player's bid and tricks taken side by side.
  • Make your bid exactly: 20 points plus 10 per trick.
  • Miss it: minus 10 per trick you were off.
  • Optional hook rule: the dealer can't make total bids equal the cards dealt.
